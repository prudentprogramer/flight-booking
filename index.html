Frequently asked questions

Important things to know

Clear answers help you decide whether this service is right for your request.

Are you an airline or an airline's official support department?

No. This page describes an independent third-party travel assistance service. It is not an airline and does not claim to represent any airline. For official airline assistance, contact the airline directly using its official website or documentation.

Will a service fee apply?

A service fee may apply depending on the assistance requested. The representative must disclose any applicable fee and explain relevant conditions before you agree to a paid service.

Can you guarantee a booking, fare, or ticket availability?

No. Fares and availability can change and depend on the booking provider. Any price, itinerary, and terms should be confirmed before a purchase is made.

Can you guarantee a cancellation or refund?

No. Refund eligibility, cancellation charges, and processing times depend on the fare rules and the relevant airline or booking provider. A request does not guarantee approval.

What information should I have ready before calling?

Have your travel dates and, if relevant, your reservation details available. Share personal information only when necessary and after you understand who is receiving it and why. Never share your account password or one-time security codes.
